In its 2024 edition, *Spend In* magazine dedicated a feature to S'Estepa under the title *"S'Estepa, al ritmo de la naturaleza"* — "S'Estepa, in tune with nature" — written by Michael Wall. The article traces how a personal passion for the Mallorcan landscape grew into a studio working across the island.

![The S'Estepa team featured in Spend In magazine 2024 — Iulia Miron, Blanca González, Pedro Campaner, Sofia Grimaux and Miguel Llabrés](/images/press/SPEND-IN-MAGAZINE-SESTEPA-LANDASCAPE-GARDEN-DESGIN-MALLORCA-Portada.jpg)

## The feature, in full

The following text was published in *Spend In*, 2024. Text by Michael Wall; photography by S'Estepa.

> Founded in 1999 by Pedro Campaner, an agricultural and landscape expert, S'Estepa has become a renowned name. With a team of fifteen gardeners, the company excels in the design, construction and maintenance of gardens and green spaces in Mallorca. Born from a dream, it has transformed into a dynamic business.
>
> At S'Estepa, the focus extends beyond just human connections; it is deeply intertwined with the land itself. The company's name echoes Pedro's deep affection for his homeland, Mallorca. The *steppe* — a plant native to the Mediterranean and renowned for its resilience — reflects the company's deep-rooted love for the island, thriving naturally in Mallorca's forests.
>
> Pedro's dream of turning his passion for gardening into both a career and a lifestyle has indeed blossomed into reality. The firm's ongoing expansion is driven by a steadfast commitment to technical excellence and ethical business practices, and is based on fostering strong relationships with its employees.
>
> Pedro and his team use realistic 3D designs to plan and maintain gardens, helping them achieve their full splendour and potential. At S'Estepa, personalised and exceptional service is central to the company's values.
